PROJECT DETAILS:
Project Name: JanSewa
Category: Open Innovation
Problem Statement: 
Local governments in Nepal face challenges in creating employment and delivering efficient community services, especially due to limited access to affordable vocational training.
Service systems are fragmented, forcing residents to rely on informal networks or external providers, causing inefficiency and economic leakage.
The lack of decentralized digital platforms and skill-building tools weakens local economies, increases migration, and limits sustainable grassroots development.
Solution Overview: 
JanSewa is a mobile-first community platform managed by local governments to connect e-governance with economic empowerment in Nepal.
It offers vocational skill enrollment and training programs in partnership with institutions like CTEVT, including certification and progress tracking.
Certified trainees and local service providers can list services in a ward-level marketplace for booking and digital payments.
Municipal authorities verify users, approve providers, monitor quality, and track economic impact to ensure transparency and accountability.
Key features include ward-based authentication, provider dashboards, geo-filtered search, admin analytics, notifications, chat support, and offline access.
